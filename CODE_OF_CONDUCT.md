# Code of conduct

This project is a place for people to talk about how software fails and how to fix it. That works only if people feel able to say "I think this is wrong" and "I do not understand" without being made to regret it.

## What we expect

- Be courteous. Criticise the code, the idea or the argument, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Be specific and patient when you disagree. A bug report or a review comment should help the reader fix something.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience, and that a basic question deserves a straight answer.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes, or attacking someone for who they are.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Spam, including promotion unrelated to the project.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone is acting on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you see behaviour that breaks these expectations, report it through GitHub. Use the contact options on the maintainer's profile at https://github.com/hasnatahmadai164, or report the content with GitHub's own tools. Say where it happened and what was said, and link to it if you can. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept private, and not shared beyond what is needed to deal with them.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock a discussion, close a pull request, or block someone from the repository. Where something is clearly a misunderstanding, a message asking the person to adjust comes first. Serious or repeated problems may lead to a ban without a warning.

## Where this comes from

This is a short statement written for a small project. The [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) is a longer and widely used alternative, and it shaped some of the wording here.
